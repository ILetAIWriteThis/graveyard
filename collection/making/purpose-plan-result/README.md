# Purpose → Plan → Result

## WHAT

## WHY

Caretaker interpretation: these three words can separate the reason for doing something, the approach being tried, and the evidence of what happened. A plan can change while its purpose survives. Naming a desired result does not make it inevitable.

For a Problem Space sketch, try three lines:

- **Purpose:** Whose problem matters, and what should improve?
- **Plan:** What approach could produce that improvement, and which assumption needs testing?
- **Result:** What observable change would count? Record the actual outcome separately once there is evidence.

The reusable piece is this small prompt. It could expose a solution chosen before the problem was understood, or distinguish a completed task from a useful outcome.
